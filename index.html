What about hosting?

With the monthly plan we take care of it. If you'd rather not pay monthly, we hand you the files and you can host it anywhere.

Can I make changes later?

Yes. Small changes are included in the monthly plan: send the photo or text on {channel} and we update it. Without the plan, we quote each change.

The preview is free. If you like it, you pay 50% upfront and the other 50% after launch. We take card, Apple Pay and Google Pay through a secure checkout.

No. We build simple one-page sites for local businesses: services, photos, reviews and a call button. We don't do online stores, customer logins or complicated sites.
